Honest limit

This dataset covers barely two full years, so the model's annual seasonal term is fit from just over one full cycle of training data — statsmodels flags this directly during fitting. Treat the forecast as directionally useful for near-term planning, not a precise year-ahead seasonal model.

Putting it together

At Risk/Churned is nearly a third of the customer base and carries a 7× churn hazard relative to Champions — the single highest-leverage retention target, not a diffuse "reduce churn" goal. Champions drive a disproportionate share of monetary value and show the least churn risk, so the forecast's near-term accuracy is implicitly a bet on that segment's continued frequency — the same purchase-frequency term that dominates the Cox model. The three analyses aren't independent exercises; segment membership is the throughline connecting who's valuable, who's leaving, and what that means for near-term revenue.
